Privacy Policy - ProPumps

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProPumps collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application for ordering building materials from factories and suppliers in Saudi Arabia.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. Name, phone number, and email address collected at registration.

Location data. With permission, used for delivery addresses and nearby suppliers. You can revoke it in device settings.

Camera and photos. With permission, used to upload images for reviews or factory partnership documents.

Order, payment, and invoice data. Orders, items, delivery address, and status. Payments run through ARB and Tamara (installments); we do not store full card numbers.

Push notifications. Firebase Cloud Messaging device tokens used to deliver order and account notifications.

Device and log information. Standard technical data such as device type and app version, for security and diagnostics.

2. How We Use Information

To run your account, process and deliver orders, communicate with you, connect you with factories, brokers, or companies you transact with, and improve the App. We do not sell personal information.

3. Sharing of Information

  • With the factory, supplier, broker, or company fulfilling your order.
  • With ARB and Tamara to complete payment.
  • With service providers such as hosting and Firebase.
  • When required by law.

4. Data Retention

Kept while your account is active or as required by Saudi tax and accounting law.

5. Your Choices

Edit your info in-app. Revoke camera, photo, or location permissions anytime in device settings. Request account deletion by emailing us, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Children

ProPumps is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted on-device token storage, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
